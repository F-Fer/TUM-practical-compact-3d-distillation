    \item 7-Scenes and DTU also under the $\pi^3$ protocol (accuracy/completeness after Sim(3) + ICP) for comparison with OTT3R \cite{ott3r} 
    \item \todo{choose subset}

\subsection{Metrics}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Alignment: point maps are aligned to the ground truth with a Sim(3) transform before scoring
    \item Point maps, unprojected from predicted depth and cameras for all methods \cite{dejaview}: relative $\ell_2$ error (Rel.\ L2) and inlier ratio (relative error $< 3\%$) \cite{dejaview}; accuracy, completeness and overall Chamfer distance on DTU/ETH3D \cite{vggt}
    \item Cameras: AUC@3$^\circ$ and AUC@30$^\circ$ of the maximum of rotation and translation angular errors over image pairs \cite{dejaview}
    \item Depth: AbsRel 
    \item Efficiency: parameters, FLOPs (forward + TTO), wall-clock runtime and peak GPU memory, on one GPU as in \cite{dejaview}
\end{itemize}
